# Commerce visual refresh — generated assets

Both images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, mode `generate`, opaque background, and converted to WebP with Sharp. They are editorial scenes, not photographs of the shipped product.

## Collection banner

Final file: `public/images/vitaboost/honey-collection-banner-v2.webp`

Exact prompt:

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide editorial photographic banner for an existing Persian honey storefront. Create a premium, warmly sunlit tabletop still life: on the LEFT 40 percent only, a small ivory ceramic bowl of luminous amber honey, a wooden honey dipper resting diagonally, a piece of real honeycomb, a casually folded natural flax linen napkin and a small sprig of white blossoms. The RIGHT 60 percent is quiet uncluttered warm ivory plaster and pale linen negative space for dark olive RTL website copy to be added in code. Wide panoramic composition, natural soft shadows from a window, shallow depth of field, beautiful real textures, restrained cream, golden amber and olive palette, luxury food editorial photography. No product packaging or jars, no logos, no lettering, no watermark, no UI, no artificial sparkles. Entire food arrangement comfortably framed with room to crop into a shallow desktop banner.

## Breakfast editorial

Final file: `public/images/vitaboost/honey-breakfast-editorial-v2.webp`

Exact prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial lifestyle image for a honey ecommerce product story panel and shopping page. A close, tactile still life of a thick slice of rustic sourdough toast on a handmade off-white plate, a small spoon trailing golden honey onto its surface, fresh figs and a rumpled beige linen napkin on an aged warm oak breakfast table. Soft early morning sunlight, deep gentle olive shadows in the background, appetizing amber highlights, realistic food textures, intimate high-end food magazine photography. Landscape composition with the toast the clear single focal point slightly to the left of center, leave a little breathing room around the scene. No people, no product packaging, no jars, no logos, no text or watermark. Warm cream, dark olive and honey gold palette consistent with a premium Persian honey shop.
